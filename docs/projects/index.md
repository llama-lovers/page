# Projects

## FastFence

A policy gateway for AI agents, models and tools. It checks requests and responses, enforces permissions and resource limits, and records decisions. Policies can be updated without restarting the gateway.

[Details](fastfence.md) · [Documentation](https://fastfence.dev/) · [GitHub](https://github.com/llama-lovers/FastFence) · [Slides](../assets/presentations/fastfence.pdf)

## FastEcho

A Chrome extension for blind and low-vision users. It accepts Polish voice commands, validates browser actions and reports the observed result through a screen reader or local speech synthesis.

[Details](fastecho.md) · [GitHub](https://github.com/llama-lovers/FastEcho) · [Demo](https://youtu.be/zODavr68b5s)

## Urban Kompas

An assistant for residents navigating municipal services in Lublin. It retrieves information from BIP sources and documents, explains the procedure and cites the source. The prototype uses RAG, OCR and local models.

[Details](urban-kompas.md) · [GitHub](https://github.com/llama-lovers/urban-kompas) · [Slides](../assets/presentations/urban-kompas.pptx)

## Shelf product detection

The Team 08 solution for Hackology II, stored in the Breathalyzer repository. It combines five prediction sources from four model weights. The presentation reports a public mAP@0.5 of 0.7420.

[Details](breathalyzer.md) · [GitHub](https://github.com/llama-lovers/breathalyzer) · [Slides](../assets/presentations/hackology.pdf)
